## Rules
1. Pool: one clip per normalised sentence (`norm_v1`), representative (no exact copies), decodable,
   no label conflict, assigned to train/val/test in S1_v0 (3378 clips).
2. Core (6 minimal-pair families, i.e. same length and exactly one word different):
   per family 4 train clips + 1 held-out sibling (half of the families give val,
   half test; families with siblings in only one split choose first). Rule 4 may swap that sibling.
3. Diversity (indicative word lists in `src/bdsl/text/tags.py`, not linguistic labels):
   train {'question': 2, 'negation': 2, 'number_time': 3, 'long': 3, 'short': 2, 'other': 4}; held-out [['val', 'question'], ['val', 'negation'], ['test', 'question'], ['test', 'negation']].
4. Held-out clips are chosen after all train clips. Prefer in-vocabulary held-out: True
   (a sentence whose words all occur in pilot train, i.e. a new combination of seen words); where none
   exists, the unseen words are listed (`unseen_in_pilot_train`). Non-sibling fallback for core slots:
   True. Result: 6/10 held-out clips use only seen words.
5. Quotas: {'landscape_min': 7, 'low_res_min': 3, 'all_duration_quartiles': True} (low-res = short side < 500 px). Candidates that fill an
   unmet quota are preferred; unmet quotas fail the selection.
6. Every choice is an ordering by SHA-256 of (seed, …): re-running gives the same 50 clips.
